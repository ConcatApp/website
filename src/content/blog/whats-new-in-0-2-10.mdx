Concat 0.2.10 is out. It adds the animations people asked for most, a way to pull a voice out of
its background, and a lot of speed. Here is what you get when you update.

## Animations: In, Out and Loop

Every clip, picture, title and shape can now arrive, leave and move while it is on screen. Open
the Animate panel and you get a wall of cards, and each card plays its move so you can see it
before you pick it. There are three pages: In for how something arrives, Out for how it leaves,
and Loop for what it does in between. Titles and captions play them too.

## Voice isolation

Pick a clip and Concat splits its sound into the voice and everything else. Keep the voice alone,
take it out, or set each part to its own level. It works on a song, where the rest is music, and
on a recording, where the rest is noise. It runs on your computer, like everything in Concat.

## Shake and motion

A new Shake & Motion shelf holds Shake, Impact, Beat Shake and Prism Shake, for the hits and
drops in an edit.

## Masks and shapes

A clip now carries a stack of masks. Each mask has its own mode and its own keyframes, so you can
add one area and cut another out of it.

Shapes are figures you edit: size, corners, polygons, stars, gradients, outlines, line ends, trim
and shadow.

## Sound

The Audio tab is rebuilt around three controls: a gain fader, a mixer for the parts of a sound,
and fades drawn on the sound itself. Fades follow a curve you can hear and see.

## Speed

| What you do                                        | Before         | After          | Faster   |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------- | -------- |
| A title or caption appears for the first time      | 77 ms          | 1.7 ms         | **45x**  |
| Open a project with 500 captions                   | about 38 s     | under 1 s      | **42x**  |
| Drag a title on a timeline with 500 captions       | 754 ms a step  | 0.8 ms a step  | **940x** |
| Show an effect for the first time after installing | 86 ms          | under 1 ms     | **150x** |
| Import a 4K clip and a 1080p clip                  | 5.0 s          | 3.3 s          | **1.5x** |

The full story of how those numbers were measured is in
[Concat got much faster](/blog/concat-got-faster/).

## Smaller things you will notice

- A new Welcome screen.
- Captions on Windows start reliably.
- Caption presets for twelve more languages: Chinese, Korean, Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hindi,
  Bengali, Thai, Vietnamese, Russian, Ukrainian and Greek.
- Generate Captions lives in the Library, under Text & Captions.
- Your computer stays awake, and your phone's screen stays on, while a job runs.
- Enhance takes any scale up to 2K and shows its progress.
- The panels and the window open where you left them.
- The speed curve graph is taller, on a labelled grid, and lets you pick several points at once.
