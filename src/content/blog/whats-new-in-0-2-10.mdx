Concat 0.2.10 is out. Things on screen can now move in, move out and loop, a voice can be
pulled out of its background, and titles and captions got a lot faster. Here is what you get
when you update.

## Animations: In, Out and Loop

Every clip, picture, title and shape can now arrive, leave and move while it is on screen.

- **In** is how something arrives.
- **Out** is how it leaves.
- **Loop** is what it does in between.

Pick a clip and open the **Animate** panel. You get a wall of cards, and each card plays its
move, so you see it before you pick it. Titles and captions play them too.

## Voice isolation

Concat can split a clip's sound into the voice and everything else. Keep the voice alone, take
it out, or set each part to its own level. On a song the rest is the music. On a recording the
rest is the noise. It all runs on your computer, like everything in Concat.

## Shake and motion

A new **Shake & Motion** shelf holds Shake, Impact, Beat Shake and Prism Shake, for the hits and
drops in an edit.

## Masks and shapes

A clip now carries a stack of masks, not just one. Each mask has its own mode and its own
keyframes, so you can keep one area, cut another out of it, and move each one on its own.

Shapes are figures you can edit: size, corners, polygons, stars, gradients, outlines, line ends,
trim and shadow.

## Sound

The **Audio** tab is rebuilt around three controls: a gain fader, a mixer for the parts of a
sound, and fades you draw on the sound itself. Fades follow a curve you can hear and see, with a
red zone on the fader and on every waveform.

## Faster

Titles and captions are now painted in the background, so they no longer freeze the window. We
measured each change before and after, on the same Mac with Apple's M5 chip and the same files:

| What you do                                        | Before        | After         | Faster   |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------- | -------- |
| A title or caption appears for the first time      | 77 ms         | 1.7 ms        | **45x**  |
| Open a project with 500 captions                   | about 38 s    | under 1 s     | **42x**  |
| Drag a title on a timeline with 500 captions       | 754 ms a step | 0.8 ms a step | **940x** |
| Show an effect for the first time after installing | 86 ms         | under 1 ms    | **150x** |
| Import a 4K clip and a 1080p clip                  | 5.0 s         | 3.3 s         | **1.5x** |
| Export 10 seconds with 10 cuts on two tracks       | 2.8 s         | 2.3 s         | **1.2x** |

Effects are ready when the window opens, so the first one you use shows at once. Filmstrips of
long footage appear sooner. The whole story is in
[Concat got much faster](/blog/concat-got-faster/).

## Captions

- Generating captions on Windows starts reliably.
- Caption presets for twelve more languages: Chinese, Korean, Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hindi,
  Bengali, Thai, Vietnamese, Russian, Ukrainian and Greek.
- **Generate Captions** now lives in the Library, under **Text & Captions**.

## Easier to use

- A new Welcome screen, with a What's new card that opens this page.
- Settings is a page over the whole window, like Export.
- Layers, effects, grades and transitions each have a colour of their own, so you can tell them
  apart at a glance.
- The panels and the window open where you left them.
- A setting that only takes effect at launch offers a restart, which saves and reopens your
  project.
- Dragging past the edge of the timeline scrolls gently or fast, depending on how far you go, and
  the scrollbar is bigger.
- OK and Cancel sit in the order your system uses.
- Your computer stays awake, and your phone's screen stays on, while a job runs.
- Enhance takes any scale up to 2K, shows its progress, and says what it will make before it
  starts.
- The speed curve graph is taller, sits on a labelled grid, and lets you pick several points at
  once. A speed curve now plays smoothly.

## Fixed

- Chroma Key works on the Windows PCs where it used to turn the preview black.
- An animation picked in the Animate panel sticks.
- The Library's sidebar scrolls, so Audio Effects is always in reach.
- The New Project sheet fits a short or narrow window.
- The missing media dialog fits its files, closes from its ×, and lets you remove each missing
  file.

## For scripts and agents

- `concat --schema` prints the whole Concat API as JSON Schema.
- An agent can give a clip a stack of masks and keyframe each one.
- `concat preview` draws an effect at any moment of its clip (`--moment`) and a transition at any
  point of its cut (`--progress`).

The [developer docs](/docs) cover every method and edit command.
