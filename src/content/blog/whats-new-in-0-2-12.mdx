Concat 0.2.12 is out. It hears the beat of your music, exports finish on phones and on Windows
PCs without a graphics driver, and nothing you make is lost on the way out. Here is what you get
when you update.

## Beats

Switch on **Beats** under **Audio › Sound** and Concat listens to your song and marks every beat
along its clips, with the first beat of each bar standing out. It all happens on your own device,
and your music never leaves it.

- The song's tempo is shown in the section.
- Clips you move snap to the beats.
- **Cut on beats** splits a clip on every beat or every bar in one step, and one undo puts it
  back.
- A video's soundtrack gets its beats too, and every file keeps its own.

It is quick. On a Mac with Apple's M5 chip, a three-minute song is heard in 1.5 seconds.

## Exports finish everywhere

On Android, an export runs to the end with the window answering all the way through, and the app
closes cleanly when it is done.

On a Windows PC without a graphics driver, Concat draws with Windows' own renderer and the export
finishes. Plain frames are drawn by Concat's own processor renderer there, which we measured on
the same machine with the same build:

| What Concat does                                  | Before  | After    | Faster   |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------- | -------- | -------- |
| Export a 1080p clip on Windows' built-in renderer | 3.6 fps | 31.1 fps | **8.6x** |

Laptops whose graphics card shares a small window of memory with the system keep their preview,
with the card's memory used where there is room.

## Nothing is lost

- A settings file is never saved over when it cannot be read.
- A voiceover still recording when you close the project is kept.
- Opening a project never drops anything from the file.
- A clip whose sound runs past its picture holds its last frame at the join.

## Treatments on the clip

The three sound treatments are switches under **Basic › Treatments** on the clip itself. While a
treatment is applied, the clip plays the sound it had until the new one is ready.

## Smaller things that got better

- A clip's width and height can be keyed, as its scale is.
- Notices are cards that wrap and stay up while you read them. A preview of a missing file names
  the file.
- On a phone, effects and transitions drop on the timeline with nothing selected.
- A mouse wheel or trackpad scrolls on Android.
- Captions hear the whole clip.
- A clip with its speed changed keeps its sound on computers that write decimals with a comma.
- A song's cover art stays out of the video.
- A quiet track's waveform keeps its shape when turned up.
- The inspector's tabs take a click during playback.
- Saves and the preview go ahead of long jobs.
- Spanish is complete, and every language has the new lines.

[Download Concat 0.2.12](https://github.com/jub0t/Concat/releases/tag/v0.2.12) for Windows,
