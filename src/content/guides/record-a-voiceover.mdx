> [!IMPORTANT]
> Not in 0.2.6. Record Voiceover is on the app's main branch and ships with the next release;
> until then the tray ends at Text to Speech.

**Record Voiceover** is the microphone button on the timeline tray, beside Text to Speech.

1. Put the playhead where the voiceover should start.
2. Click **Record Voiceover**. The timeline's own sound is muted, playback starts from the
   playhead, the button turns red and reads **Stop Recording**, and a level meter runs beside it.
3. Talk.
4. Click **Stop Recording**.

The take is saved as a WAV in your project's `audio` folder, added to the Library, and placed as
a clip on the first free lane at the point where recording began, nudged by the microphone's
latency so it lines up. A toast says "Voiceover added to the timeline".

There is no device picker: Concat records from the system's default input. Change that in your
operating system's sound settings before you press the button.

## After the take

- Too quiet, or a noisy room: select the clip, open the Inspector's **Audio** tab and turn on
  **Normalize loudness**, **Reduce noise** or **Enhance voice**. See
  [clean up a voice](/guides/clean-up-a-voice).
- Want captions for it: select the clip and click **Generate Captions**.
- A flubbed line: put the playhead on it, press <kbd class="kbd">⌘B</kbd> to split, delete the
  piece, record again.
