**Record Voiceover** is the microphone button on the timeline tray, beside Text to Speech. It
opens the **Voiceover** panel, and a second press puts it away (or stops a take that is running).

## Record a take

1. Put the playhead where the voiceover should start.
2. Click **Record Voiceover**, check the meter, and press **Record**.
3. Talk.
4. Press **Stop**.

The take is saved as a WAV in your project's `audio` folder, added to the Library, and placed as a
clip where recording began, and a toast says "Voiceover added to the timeline".

## The Voiceover panel

The panel floats over the editor without dimming it, so the picture, the lanes and the clips stay usable between takes. Drag it by its header. On a
phone it is a sheet at the foot of the screen, opened from **Voiceover** on the bottom strip.

The panel opens the microphone straight away, so its meter shows the room before you say a word:
the bar is the level, a tick marks the peak, and **CLIP** lights when the input reaches full
scale ("Clipping: turn the input gain down").

| Control                           | What it does                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Microphone                        | Picks the input from the system's list                                                                |
| **Input gain**                    | How loud the microphone is recorded                                                                   |
| **Count in 3 seconds**            | Three seconds before the take starts                                                                  |
| **Mute timeline while recording** | Keeps the timeline's sound out of your headphones and the take                                        |
| **Record**                        | Rolls the picture from the playhead and records                                                       |
| **Pause**, **Resume**             | Holds the picture and the take together, then carries both on, so the take stays in step              |
| **Stop**                          | Lands the take on the lane the last take went on when it is free, otherwise the first free lane       |
| **Retake**                        | Throws away the take that is running, or the one just landed, and goes again from where it began      |
| **Teleprompter**                  | A script box that scrolls while you record, at **Slow**, **Steady** or **Fast**, and holds on a pause |

The microphone, the gain, the count-in and the mute switch are remembered between runs. A Retake
of a landed take is one undo step, so <kbd class="kbd">⌘Z</kbd> brings it back.

While a take runs it owns the transport: <kbd class="kbd">Space</kbd> and the monitor's Play
pause and resume the take and the picture together, and the playhead stays with the take until
you stop it. Closing the panel mid-take stops the take and keeps it.

The take is written to disk as it goes, so a crash leaves a file that plays. A microphone that
only hands over silence gets a note: allow Concat under **Privacy & Security › Microphone** in
the system settings. On Android the panel asks for the microphone permission and offers **Try
again** once it is granted.

## After the take

- Too quiet, or a noisy room: select the clip, open the Inspector's **Audio** tab and turn on
  **Normalize loudness**, **Reduce noise** or **Enhance voice**. See
  [clean up a voice](/guides/clean-up-a-voice).
- Want captions for it: select the clip and click **Generate Captions**.
- A flubbed line: press **Retake** in the panel. Or put the playhead on it, press
  <kbd class="kbd">⌘B</kbd> to split, delete the piece and record again.
