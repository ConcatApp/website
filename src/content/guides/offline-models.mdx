Nothing uploads. Captions, voices and cutout all run on your machine, on models that download from
Settings the first time you use them and never need the network again. Your footage never leaves
your disk.

## Auto-captions

Local Whisper transcribes the audio. Pick a model size, get styled captions on the timeline. The
model files run from 78 MB to 488 MB depending on the size you pick; a bigger model is more
accurate and takes longer, and the larger ones want 16 GB of RAM.

## Text-to-speech and voice cloning

Free local voices, or any voice from a few seconds of a recording. The voice models are 132 MB or
349 MB.

## Background removal

Cut out people, cut out objects, or paint the mask yourself. Person cutout is 15 MB, object cutout
179 MB, and the cutout brush 40 MB.

## Voice cleanup

One switch to denoise, enhance the voice and level the loudness. Plus chipmunk, robot, telephone
and friends. These need no download.

> [!TIP]
> Storage: 500 MB covers the app and the smallest caption model. Reserve 2 GB if you want every
> optional model installed. See [System requirements](/guides/system-requirements).
