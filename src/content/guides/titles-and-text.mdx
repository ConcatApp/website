## Add a title

Open the Library's **Text** page. Its left column has three shelves:

- **Titles**: one card, **Default title**. Click it and a plain title lands at the playhead on
  the first free lane above the picture. Drag it onto a lane instead to choose where it goes.
- **Styles**: ready-made looks. Click one and the title arrives already styled.
- **Languages**: the same, for scripts that need their own font (Japanese, Arabic, Hindi and
  so on); the font is installed with the look.

On a phone, **Text** on the bottom strip has **Add a title** and the same looks.

## Write the words

Select the title and type into the **Content** box at the top of the Inspector's **Text** tab.
Press <kbd class="kbd">Return</kbd> for a new line. The monitor updates as you type.

## Place it

Drag the title on the monitor to move it; drag a corner grip to scale it; drag the stem above
the box to rotate it. <kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd> while dragging snaps to the guides. The
**Position** section at the bottom of the Text tab holds the same two numbers, **Horizontal**
and **Vertical**, if you prefer typing.

## Style it

The Text tab, top to bottom:

| Section        | Controls                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Typography** | Font (with search, and **Import a font…** for a file of your own), Font Weight (Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold), Size, Italic, Color, Opacity, **Drop shadow**, **Background** and its colour, opacity, corner radius and padding |
| **Stroke**     | Width, Color, Opacity                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Layout**     | Text Align, Width and Height (a number or Auto), Line height, Tracking                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Position**   | Horizontal, Vertical                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

Two of these do the heavy lifting for titles that sit over footage:

- **Drop shadow** is a soft shadow under the words, for titles that have to sit over footage you
  do not control.
- **Background** is a solid box behind the words. Where a shadow softens the footage, a plate
  replaces it; use it when what is underneath is busy. The plate takes a colour, an opacity, a
  corner radius and padding, and goes round-ended when the plate is pill-shaped.

A title with no box wraps at the frame's edge instead of running off it.

> [!NOTE]
> **Fixed in 0.2.8 (coming in the next release).** In 0.2.7 a title with a box wider than half
> the frame, aligned left or right, is cut off at its far end; centred titles are not affected.
> 0.2.8 slides the box back onto the frame as far as it must. 0.2.8 also draws titles and captions
> in Thai, Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hindi, Bengali and Korean on Android and iOS, where 0.2.7 shows
> boxes for them.

## Style several at once

Select more than one title (<kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd>-click, or drag a box around them on
the lane) and the Text tab reads "Editing N titles". Font, size, colours, stroke, shadow,
background, box and alignment apply to all of them in one undo step. Position, rotation and
scale move them together and keep their spacing.

## Make it the default

Click **Use for new titles**, under the Font picker. From then on every new title and every
generated caption starts in that look. Set a CJK face once and it is used from then on.

## Fonts

The Font picker lists the bundled faces, any fonts you have imported, then the machine's fonts,
with a search box at the top. A title whose font is not installed on this machine shows **FONT
NOT FOUND** and draws in a fallback; install the font or pick another. Imported fonts live with
Concat, so a project that uses one opens the same way on another machine once the font is
imported there too.

## Shapes

The Library's **Stickers** page has a **Shapes** shelf: Square, Circle, Triangle,
Parallelogram, Trapezoid, Line and Arrow. Click to place one at the playhead; it gets the same
Transform and Blend controls as a picture, under a **Vector Image** tab.
