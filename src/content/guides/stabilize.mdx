> [!NOTE]
> **New in 0.2.8 (coming in the next release).** Stabilize is on the app's main branch and not
> in 0.2.7.

Select a video clip and open the Inspector's **Video** tab, **Basic** page. Tick **Stabilize** in
its section header. On a phone, the clip's **Edit** sheet has the same section.

## What happens when you tick it

Concat measures the camera's movement in the clip's file once, in the background, and the
section shows "Measuring the shake… N%". Until the measurement lands the clip plays as shot;
then the monitor shows it steadied. Only the part of the file the clip uses is measured, plus a
little either side for the smoothing, and a measurement already made is reused by every clip of
the same file. Nothing needs downloading and nothing leaves your machine.

## The three controls

| Control        | What it does                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Smoothness** | How much of the camera's movement is smoothed away. Low keeps a pan or a walk and takes out the jitter; high leaves only the slowest moves. Starts at 50% |
| **Max zoom**   | How far the picture may be enlarged to hide the edges the correction pulls into view, up to 150%. Starts at 120%                                          |
| **Tripod**     | Holds the frame completely still, as if the camera had been on a tripod, instead of following the camera's intended move                                  |

The sliders answer at once: the correction is worked out live from the measurement, so there is
no second wait.

Once measured, the section says what zoom the clip is drawn at ("Zoomed to 112% to hide the
edges"). When Max zoom holds the correction back, it says how much zoom steadying every frame
would take; raise Max zoom to steady the shakiest moments fully, or accept a little movement
there.

## Export and the API

An export measures any stabilized clip that has not been measured yet before it renders, so a
file always comes out steadied. The monitor and the export draw the clip through the same
correction. Scripts set it with the `setClipStabilize` edit; see
[Edit commands](/docs/api/edits#setclipstabilize).

## If Concat closed while measuring

The project still opens: the measurement it died in is skipped and a notice says so once. Turn
Stabilize off and on again to retry.

A [background removal](/guides/remove-a-background) painted on a stabilized clip lands where you
painted it.
