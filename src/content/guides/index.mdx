Concat is a video editor that runs entirely on your machine. Download it, open it, drop footage
in, cut. No account, no setup. These pages answer the questions people actually ask: where the
text-to-speech button is, how to get captions, how to get a clean export out.

Every screenshot is the real app, in its dark theme, on the current release.

<GuideList />

Building on the API instead? The [developer docs](/docs) cover the API, JSON-RPC and gRPC, and
the MCP server that is planned.

Pages and sections marked **New in 0.2.8 (coming in the next release)** describe what is on the
app's main branch and not yet in a release. Everything else is the current release, 0.2.7.
