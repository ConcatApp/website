Open Settings from the **gear** at the top right of the window (it is on the start screen too),
with <kbd class="kbd">⌘,</kbd> (<kbd class="kbd">Ctrl+,</kbd>), or from File › Settings. The
pages run down the left side.

In 0.2.7 the pages are **General**, **Appearance**, **Transcriber**, **Speech**, **Remote**,
**Version** and **About**. 0.2.8 adds **Shortcuts** after Appearance and renames Remote to
**Automation**. On a phone, or a window narrower than 600 pixels, 0.2.8 shows Settings full
screen with its pages as a row of tabs.

## General

- **Language**: the 25 the app ships in, each in its own name.
- **Log level**: Errors only, Warnings, Normal, Debug, Everything. Normal is enough for most
  reports; Debug or Everything when a report asks for it, and the file grows faster.
  `CONCAT_LOG` in the environment overrides it for one run.
- **Downloads**: where a model is fetched from first. Automatic, Concat's mirror, a mirror for
  mainland China, the original source, or a custom URL of your own. Every other source is still
  tried after the one you pick.
- **Magnetic timeline**: whether deleting and trimming close the gap. The same switch is on
  the tray.
- **Playhead**: on, the playhead never leaves the content; off, a click on the ruler past the
  last clip lands it there.

> [!NOTE]
> **New in 0.2.8 (coming in the next release).** Two more switches, under the others:
>
> - **Proxies**, off by default. Off, playback reads your files as they are. On, each video
>   larger than HD gets a smaller copy, made once in the background, and Half and Quarter play
>   from it. In 0.2.7 every video larger than HD gets a proxy as soon as it is imported.
> - **Draw on the GPU** (Windows and Linux). Off, the window and the preview draw in software:
>   slower, but out of the way of a graphics driver that closes Concat. It takes effect the next
>   time Concat starts. If Concat closes while it is starting, the next launch turns it off by
>   itself and says so on the start screen; turn it back on here to try the graphics card again.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: Dark or Light. Dark is the default.
- **Accent**: Lime, Sky, Violet, Rose, Amber, Mint, or a Custom colour.
- **Playhead**: the colour of the line across the lanes.

> [!NOTE]
> **New in 0.2.8 (coming in the next release).** **Scale** draws the interface larger: System,
> 100%, 110%, 125%, 150%, 175% or 200%. It multiplies the display's own scale, so 125% on a
> Retina Mac or a Windows laptop at 150% makes everything a quarter larger than it already was,
> and a window moved to a monitor of another scale follows. Desktop only.

## Shortcuts

> [!NOTE]
> **New in 0.2.8 (coming in the next release).**

Every keyboard shortcut, with the keys it answers to. Click a row and press new keys to rebind
it; **Reset** and **Reset all** put the shipped keys back. See
[keyboard shortcuts](/guides/keyboard-shortcuts#change-a-shortcut). Not on phones.

## Transcriber

The Whisper models that auto-captions use, one row each: Tiny, Base and Small, each in an
English and a Multilingual variant, with a line on what it is good for, accuracy pips and the
size. **Download** fetches one; **Remove** deletes it; clicking an installed row makes it the one
**In use**. Under the list: what is installed and how much disk it takes, and "Runs locally".
See [the AI models](/guides/offline-models).

## Speech

The voice models that text to speech uses: **Chatterbox Turbo (studio)**, **Pocket TTS
(cloning)**, **Kokoro (compact)** and **Kokoro (full precision)**, with the same Download,
Remove and In use controls.

## Remote (Automation from 0.2.8)

Serves the Concat API while the window is open, so a script or another program can create, edit
and export projects of its own. In 0.2.7: a **Remote control** switch, an **Address** (127.0.0.1
is this machine only; any other address is reachable from the network and needs the token), and
a **Token** that a connection must present before its first call, with Show, Hide and New. The
[developer docs](/docs) cover what to send it. Not on phones.

> [!NOTE]
> **New in 0.2.8 (coming in the next release).** The page is called **Automation** and its switch
> **Scripts and apps**. A dot beside the server's status line is green while it listens, red when
> it could not start and dim when it is off. The address and port are one field; an address past
> this computer gets a warning that the network can reach it, with **This computer only** to put
> 127.0.0.1 back. The token stays hidden until you show it, and **Copy the token** copies it
> without showing it.

## Version

The installed version and whether a newer release exists, with **Check for updates**. **Switch
version** lists every release from 0.2.5 on, newer or older, and **Install** puts the one you
pick in this one's place. See [update Concat](/guides/update). Not on phones.

## About

The version, **Show the log** and **Copy system information**, then the facts a bug report
needs: application, build, engine, operating system, processor, graphics, renderer, toolchain.
From 0.2.8 an Intel Mac on macOS 12 also sees an **AI features** line saying they need macOS
13.4. See [report a problem](/guides/report-a-problem).
