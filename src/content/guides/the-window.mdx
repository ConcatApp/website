## The layout

A fresh window opens with the **Inspector** as a full-height column down the right side, about a
fifth of the width. In the rest, the **Library** (top left, a quarter of the window) and the
**Preview** sit across the top, with the **Timeline** under both in the lower two fifths. The
level meters are part of the timeline, at its right edge. The screenshot above is the 0.2.7
layout, with the Inspector stopping at the timeline; panes move as described below either way.

## The title strip

<Figure
  src={strip}
  alt="The right end of the title strip: the Add a panel button, the Settings gear and the Export button"
  width="32rem"
>
  At the right end of the strip: **Add a panel**, the **Settings** gear, and **Export**.
</Figure>

The strip at the very top carries the **File**, **Edit** and **View** menus on the left, the
project's name and its save state in the middle ("saved" almost all the time: the edit autosaves
a second and a half after every change), and three controls on the right:

- **Add a panel** (the plus with a chevron) opens a list of the pane kinds (Library, Preview,
  Inspector, Keyframes and Timeline) and adds one.
- The **gear** opens Settings. It is the only door to Settings besides
  <kbd class="kbd">⌘,</kbd> and File › Settings.
- **Export** opens the export sheet.

On Windows and Linux the strip also draws the window buttons, and the menus open from the keyboard
with <kbd class="kbd">F10</kbd>, <kbd class="kbd">Alt+F</kbd>, <kbd class="kbd">Alt+E</kbd> and <kbd class="kbd">Alt+V</kbd>.

**File** has Open project, Import media, Save, Export, Save as template, **Text to speech**,
Clear project cache, Settings, Close project and Close window. **Edit** has Undo, Redo, Split at
playhead, Delete clip, and the two timeline switches, **Snap to edges** and **Magnetic
timeline**. **View** has Zoom in and out, the three sort orders for the bin, and Go to start
and end.

## The Library

<Figure
  src={tabs}
  alt="The Library's page tabs: Media, Text, Stickers, Transitions, Effects, Filters, Templates"
  width="28rem"
>
  The Library's pages as 0.2.7 drew them. 0.2.8 adds Audio after Media and Adjustments after
  Filters.
</Figure>

The Library has nine pages across its top: **Media** (your files, filtered by All media,
Video, Audio, Images, and the Generated shelves Speech and Processed), **Audio** (your own sounds,
kept across projects, and your voices; see [sounds and voices](/guides/sounds-and-voices)), **Text**
(titles and the Styles and Languages looks), **Stickers** (shapes), **Transitions**, **Effects**
(video and audio), **Filters** (colour looks), **Adjustments** (an adjustment layer and your
LUTs, with Import a LUT) and **Templates**. Each page has a column of categories on its left and
cards on the right. The [library guide](/guides/library) explains what a click, a double-click
and a drag do on each page, because they differ.

## The Preview

<Figure
  src={monitor}
  alt="The monitor's transport row: timecode, duration, go to start, previous frame, play, next frame, go to end, the frame picker, the quality picker and fit"
  width="40rem"
>
  Under the picture: the timecode and duration, transport buttons, then the frame-shape picker, the
  quality picker (Full, Half, Quarter) and Fit.
</Figure>

The monitor shows the frame at the playhead. Click a clip's picture to select it; drag the box
to move it, drag a grip to scale it, drag the stem above the box to rotate it.

<kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd> while dragging snaps to the guides. Right-click the picture for **Save
Video Frame As**, which writes the frame as a PNG and drops it in the bin.

**Half** quality is the default. Proxies, smaller copies of every video larger than HD that keep
4K smooth at Half and Quarter, are a switch in Settings › General, off by default; with it off,
every quality reads the files themselves. A single click on a file in the Library shows that file
here instead of the timeline, with a "Source" chip; click the timeline to come back.

With a clip's [mask](/guides/mask) open in the Inspector, the monitor outlines it with grips to
move, size, turn and soften it.

## The Inspector

With nothing selected the Inspector shows the project's **Details** and a **Modify** button for
the timeline's frame, rate and colour. With a clip selected it shows tabs for that kind of clip:

| Clip                     | Tabs                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Video                    | **Video** (Basic, Enhance, Adjust), **Audio** (Basic, Audio effects), **Effects** |
| Image                    | **Video** (Basic, Enhance, Adjust), **Effects**                                   |
| Audio                    | **Audio** (Basic, Audio effects)                                                  |
| Several clips with sound | **Audio**, with the volume only                                                   |
| Title                    | **Text**                                                                          |
| Filter or adjustment     | **Filter** (Layer for its strength and ramps, Adjust for its colour, Effects)     |
| Shape                    | **Vector Image**                                                                  |

Every knob with a small diamond next to it can be keyframed at the playhead. Double-click a
label to put a knob back to its default.

The Video tab's Basic page holds, besides the transform, the **Canvas** section and the Fit and
Fill buttons ([vertical video](/guides/vertical-video)), **Stabilize**
([stabilize](/guides/stabilize)), **Remove background**
([remove a background](/guides/remove-a-background)) and **Mask** ([mask](/guides/mask)). The
Audio tab's two pages are covered in [clean up a voice](/guides/clean-up-a-voice), the Enhance
page in [enhance](/guides/enhance).

Above the tray sit the **timeline tabs**. A project can hold several timelines, each with its own
frame and rate; the plus adds one, a double-click renames one. Right-click a clip for its menu:
Copy, Duplicate, Paste, Split at playhead, Delete left, Delete right, Merge selected clips, Freeze frame,
Enhance, Fit to frame, Fill frame, Detach audio (or Reattach), Save Audio As, Generate captions,
Mute, Lock track and Delete.

## Moving panes around

Every pane's header ends with a small chip. Click it to change what that pane shows, or to
remove the pane. Drag the chip onto another pane: drop near an edge to split that pane, drop in
the middle to swap the two. The **Keyframes** kind is how you get the dopesheet and curve editor
on screen; see [keyframes](/guides/keyframes).

## Closing

The edit autosaves, so closing the window is safe. If the last save fails (an unplugged drive, a
full disk, a read-only folder), Concat asks **Quit without saving?** instead of keeping the window
open with no way out; cancel to try again once the disk is back.

A project is open in one Concat at a time. A second window, or an AI agent working on its own,
that tries to open a project already open is refused with "This project is open in another Concat
(process N). Close it there, then open it here."
