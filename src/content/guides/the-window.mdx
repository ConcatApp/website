## The title strip

<Figure
  src={strip}
  alt="The right end of the title strip: the Add a panel button, the Settings gear and the Export button"
  width="32rem"
>
  At the right end of the strip: **Add a panel**, the **Settings** gear, and **Export**.
</Figure>

The strip at the very top carries the **File**, **Edit** and **View** menus on the left, the
project's name and its save state in the middle ("saved" almost all the time: the edit autosaves
a second and a half after every change), and three controls on the right:

- **Add a panel** (the plus with a chevron) opens a list of the six pane kinds and adds one.
- The **gear** opens Settings. It is the only door to Settings besides
  <kbd class="kbd">⌘,</kbd> and File › Settings.
- **Export** opens the export sheet.

On Windows and Linux the strip also draws the window buttons, and

<kbd class="kbd">F10</kbd>, <kbd class="kbd">Alt+F</kbd>, <kbd class="kbd">Alt+E</kbd> and
<kbd class="kbd">Alt+V</kbd> open the menus from the keyboard.

**File** has Open project, Import media, Save, Export, Save as template, **Text to speech**,
Clear project cache, Settings, Close project and Close window. **Edit** has Undo, Redo, Split at
playhead, Delete clip, and the two timeline switches, **Snap to edges** and **Magnetic
timeline**. **View** has Zoom in and out, the three sort orders for the bin, and Go to start
and end.

## The Library

<Figure
  src={tabs}
  alt="The Library's page tabs: Media, Text, Stickers, Transitions, Effects, Filters, Templates"
  width="28rem"
>
  The Library's pages.
</Figure>

The Library has seven pages across its top: **Media** (your files, filtered by All media,
Video, Audio, Images, and the Generated shelves Speech and Processed), **Text** (titles and the
Styles and Languages looks), **Stickers** (shapes), **Transitions**, **Effects** (video and
audio), **Filters** (colour looks, with Import a LUT) and **Templates**. Each page has a column
of categories on its left and cards on the right. The
[library guide](/guides/library) explains what a click, a double-click and a drag do on each
page, because they differ.

## The Preview

<Figure
  src={monitor}
  alt="The monitor's transport row: timecode, duration, go to start, previous frame, play, next frame, go to end, the frame picker, the quality picker and fit"
  width="40rem"
>
  Under the picture: the timecode and duration, transport buttons, then the frame-shape picker, the
  quality picker (Full, Half, Quarter) and Fit.
</Figure>

The monitor shows the frame at the playhead. Click a clip's picture to select it; drag the box
to move it, drag a grip to scale it, drag the stem above the box to rotate it.

<kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd> while dragging snaps to the guides. Right-click the picture for **Save
Video Frame As**, which writes the frame as a PNG and drops it in the bin.

**Half** quality is the default: it plays proxies, which keep 4K smooth. **Full** plays the
original files. A single click on a file in the Library shows that file here instead of the
timeline, with a "Source" chip; click the timeline to come back.

## The Inspector

With nothing selected the Inspector shows the project's **Details** and a **Modify** button for
the timeline's frame, rate and colour. With a clip selected it shows tabs for that kind of clip:

| Clip   | Tabs                                                               |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Video  | **Video** (Basic, Enhance, Adjust), **Audio** (Sound), **Effects** |
| Image  | **Video** (Basic, Enhance, Adjust), **Effects**                    |
| Audio  | **Audio** (Sound)                                                  |
| Title  | **Text**                                                           |
| Filter | **Filter** (the layer's strength and ramps)                        |
| Shape  | **Vector Image**                                                   |

Every knob with a small diamond next to it can be keyframed at the playhead. Double-click a
label to put a knob back to its default.

Above the tray sit the **timeline tabs**. A project can hold several timelines, each with its own
frame and rate; the plus adds one, a double-click renames one. Right-click a clip for its menu:
Copy, Duplicate, Paste, Split at playhead, Freeze frame, Enhance, Detach audio, Save Audio As,
Mute, Lock track and Delete.

## Moving panes around

Every pane's header ends with a small chip. Click it to change what that pane shows, or to
remove the pane. Drag the chip onto another pane: drop near an edge to split that pane, drop in
the middle to swap the two. The sixth kind, **Keyframes**, is how you get the dopesheet and curve
editor on screen; see [keyframes](/guides/keyframes).
