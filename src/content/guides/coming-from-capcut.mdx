Concat is a free, open-source video editor built around the edits people open CapCut for:
auto-captions, text to speech, background removal, keyframes, effects, titles and a multi-track
timeline. It runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iPhone. The difference is where it runs
and what it asks of you: everything happens on your machine, and there is nothing to sign up for.

## What changes when you switch

- **No account.** Open the app and start a project. There is no login, no email and nothing
  to renew.
- **No watermark, ever.** Every export is clean at every resolution, up to 4K 60. There is no
  paid tier to unlock and no "Pro" badge on any effect.
- **No upload.** Your footage, your voice and your captions never leave your disk. The AI
  features run locally: their models download once from Settings and work offline after that.
- **Open source.** The code is public under the AGPL-3.0 licence, on
  [GitHub](https://github.com/jub0t/Concat).

What you give up: Concat has no stock music library yet (its own sound
shelf reads "Sound library coming soon"), and it is in beta. It works, and it still has edges;
[report them](/guides/report-a-problem) when you find one.

## Where the familiar tools are

| You want to                      | In Concat                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Add auto-captions                | The **T** button on the timeline tray. [Auto-captions](/guides/auto-captions)                       |
| Turn text into a voiceover       | The speaker button next to it. [Text to speech](/guides/text-to-speech)                             |
| Clone a voice                    | The same sheet, with a few seconds of a recording. [Voice cloning](/guides/text-to-speech)          |
| Remove a background              | Inspector, **Video** tab, **Remove background**. [Remove a background](/guides/remove-a-background) |
| Add text and titles              | The Library's **Text** page. [Titles and text](/guides/titles-and-text)                             |
| Effects, filters and transitions | The Library's pages of the same names, 100 of them. [The library guide](/guides/library)            |
| Animate with keyframes           | The diamond beside any knob in the Inspector. [Keyframes](/guides/keyframes)                        |
| Blur the bars around a wide clip | Inspector, **Video**, **Canvas**, **Blur**. [Vertical video](/guides/vertical-video)                |

| Export                           | The blue **Export** button at the top right. [Export](/guides/export)                               |

## The main track works the way you expect

The lowest lane that holds a picture is the **main lane**, where CapCut has its main track. The
canvas background (black, a blur of the clip, a colour or a picture) is drawn behind it, and
titles, stickers and overlays go on the lanes above. With the **Magnetic timeline** switch on,
deleting a clip closes the gap behind it. Picture-in-picture is a clip on a lane above the main
one, sized and placed in the Inspector.

## One project, several cuts

A Concat project can hold several timelines, each with its own frame and rate, so a 16:9 YouTube
cut and a 9:16 vertical cut of the same footage live side by side as tabs. Export whichever is
active. See [several timelines](/guides/cut-on-the-timeline#several-timelines).

## On a phone too

The Android and iPhone builds have their own phone layout, with the tools on a strip along the
bottom like the editors you know, and the same project files as the desktop. See
[Concat on a phone](/guides/phone).
