Select a clip with sound on the timeline and open the Inspector's **Audio** tab. It has two pages
on a strip at its top, as the Video tab does: **Basic** (the level, the fades, a sound
clip's speed and the three treatments) and **Audio effects** (the effect chain and its library).
Applying an audio effect from the Library opens Audio effects. On a phone, **Audio effects** sits
next to **Audio** on the clip's strip.

## The three treatments

Each is a switch with a line explaining what it does:

- **Normalize loudness**: brings the clip to a broadcast level without riding the fader. Turn
  this on for every voice track and stop worrying about levels.
- **Reduce noise**: takes the room's hiss and hum out from under the voice.
- **Enhance voice**: lifts speech out of the mix, a little presence, a little control.

They need no model download and apply as the clip plays. All three on is the one-switch cleanup
the README promises.

## Volume without the inspector

Every clip with sound wears a thin horizontal line at its level on the timeline. Drag it to set
the volume; it snaps at 100 percent and the drag is one undo step.

## The volume of several clips at once

Select two or more clips that all have sound (audio clips, or videos with sound) and the
Inspector shows only the **Audio** tab with the volume. Set it and every selected clip takes that
level, as one undo step. A clip whose volume has keys keeps its ride: the whole ride moves by the
same number of decibels, so the level at the playhead lands where you set it and a fade stays a
fade. Clips on locked tracks are left alone. On a phone, the clip strip's **Audio** does the same
for the selection.

## The effect chain

The **Audio effects** page holds the clip's **audio effect chain** and a library to pick from.
The Library pane's **Effects** page lists the same ones under **Audio Effects**, grouped by
category:

- **Voice**: Chipmunk, Deep, Robot, Telephone, Megaphone, Whisper, Sweet, Radio, Lo-fi, Vinyl,
  Underwater, Pitch.
- **Space**: Room, Hall, Plate, Cathedral, Stadium, Echo.
- **Tone**: Bass, Treble, Bright, Distorted.

Click one to add it to the selected clip. The chain runs top to bottom; reorder with the
chevrons, bypass one with its eye, and dial its knobs underneath. A knob with a diamond can be
keyframed.

## Pitch and speed

**Rate** re-times the clip in place, so the timeline length changes with it; trim afterwards.
**Change pitch with speed** is off by default, so a sped-up voice stays at its pitch; turn it on
for the chipmunk-by-speed effect.
