Shortcuts are shown in the menus and in every tray button's tooltip, with the right keys for the
platform you are on. Single-key shortcuts do nothing while you are typing in a text field.

## Change a shortcut

**Settings › Shortcuts** lists every action with the keys it answers to. Click a row and press
the keys it should take; <kbd class="kbd">Escape</kbd> cancels. If another action already has
those keys, they move to the new one and the old action is left with no key ("… is unbound now")
until its row's **Reset** gives it back. **Reset all** puts every key back as shipped. The
menus and the tray's tooltips show the keys as they are bound now.

A few chords belong to the window and are refused ("That chord belongs to the window"):

<kbd class="kbd">⌘O</kbd> and <kbd class="kbd">⌘W</kbd> everywhere, and on Windows and Linux
<kbd class="kbd">Ctrl+Q</kbd>, <kbd class="kbd">F10</kbd> and <kbd class="kbd">Alt+F</kbd>,
<kbd class="kbd">Alt+E</kbd>, <kbd class="kbd">Alt+V</kbd>. The Shortcuts page is a desktop one; a
phone has no keyboard to bind.

The tables below are the keys Concat ships with.

| Add to the selection          | <kbd class="kbd">⇧</kbd> or <kbd class="kbd">⌘</kbd> click                      | <kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd> or <kbd class="kbd">Ctrl</kbd> click                           |

**Split at playhead** cuts the selected clips, and every clip under the playhead only when nothing
is selected. **Delete left** and **Delete right** are CapCut's keys for the same thing: they cut the selected clip from its start to the playhead, or from the
playhead to its end, and close the gap on a magnetic timeline. See
[cut on the timeline](/guides/cut-on-the-timeline#delete-left-and-right).

## Playback and the lanes

| Action                   | Key                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Play, pause              | <kbd class="kbd">Space</kbd>                                                                                                          |
| One frame back, forward  | <kbd class="kbd">←</kbd>, <kbd class="kbd">→</kbd>; with <kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd> ten frames                                      |
| Go to start, end         | <kbd class="kbd">Home</kbd>, <kbd class="kbd">End</kbd>                                                                               |
| Snap to edges on and off | <kbd class="kbd">N</kbd>                                                                                                              |
| Preview axis on and off  | <kbd class="kbd">S</kbd>                                                                                                              |
| Pan tool on and off      | <kbd class="kbd">H</kbd>                                                                                                              |
| Zoom the lanes           | <kbd class="kbd">⌘=</kbd> or <kbd class="kbd">⌘+</kbd>, <kbd class="kbd">⌘-</kbd>; <kbd class="kbd">⌘</kbd> + wheel about the pointer |
| Scroll time              | <kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd> + wheel, or a horizontal wheel                                                                           |

## The monitor

| Action              | macOS                                         | Windows and Linux                               |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Snap while dragging | hold <kbd class="kbd">⇧</kbd>                 | hold <kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd>               |
| Pan the view        | <kbd class="kbd">⌥</kbd> drag, or middle drag | <kbd class="kbd">Alt</kbd> drag, or middle drag |
| Zoom the view       | <kbd class="kbd">⌘</kbd> + wheel              | <kbd class="kbd">Ctrl</kbd> + wheel             |
| Fit                 | double-click the ground                       | double-click the ground                         |

<kbd class="kbd">Escape</kbd> closes the sheet or dialog on top (Settings, Export, New project,
Captions and the rest), except one busy with a job.

Shortcuts keep working with Caps Lock on. There is no J, K, L shuttle and no I and O for in and
out points yet.
