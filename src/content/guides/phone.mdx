On a phone, and in any window narrower than about 860 pixels, Concat shows a compact editor
rather than a shrunken desktop. Same engine, same project files, same AI features.

## The layout

Top to bottom: a **top bar** (close project on the left, the project's name in the middle, the
Settings gear and **Export** on the right), the **monitor**, a row with Fit, **Undo** and
**Redo**, a round **Play** button, the **lanes** with a centred playhead (drag the lanes to
scrub), Zoom out and in, and the **bottom strip** of tools.

## The bottom strip

With nothing selected: **Media**, **Text**, **Effects**, **Filters**, **Transitions**,
**Captions**, **Speak**, **Project**. Each opens a sheet that slides up; swipe down to close it.

- **Media**: an **Import media** button and the bin. Tap a file to put it on the timeline at
  the playhead.
- **Text**: **Add a title**, or a title in one of the looks.
- **Effects**, **Filters**, **Transitions**: a search field and the catalogue. Effects and
  transitions go on the selected clip, so select one first.
- **Captions** and **Speak**: the same Captions and Text to speech sheets as the desktop. See
  [auto-captions](/guides/auto-captions) and [text to speech](/guides/text-to-speech).
- **Project**: the project's facts and a **Modify** button for the frame and rate.

With a clip selected the strip changes to **Back**, **Split**, **Delete**, then the tools for
that kind of clip: **Edit** (transform, blend, remove background), **Text**, **Filter**,
**Speed**, **Audio**, **Adjust**, **Effects**, **Transitions**, **Duplicate**, **Freeze**,
**Reverse**, **Mirror**, **Rotate**, **Captions**, **Speak**, and **More** for the rest of the
clip menu.

## What is different

- Split cuts the selected clip at the playhead; there is no razor.
- Undo and Redo are the two buttons beside the monitor.
- Settings has no Remote and no Version page. Updates come from the APK or IPA on GitHub
  Releases.
- The start screen has no Open project and no location picker on Android: projects live where
  the app puts them.
- Export has no Browse: the file goes to the app's Movies folder, and sharing it is the system
  share sheet.

## Install

Android: the APK from the [download picker](/#download); allow installs from this source the
first time. iOS and iPadOS: a beta IPA, sideloaded. See [install](/guides/install).
