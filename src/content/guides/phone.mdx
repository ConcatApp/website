On a phone, and in any window narrower than about 860 pixels, Concat shows a compact editor
rather than a shrunken desktop. Same engine, same project files, same AI features.

## The layout

Top to bottom: a **top bar** (close project on the left, the project's name in the middle, the
Settings gear and **Export** on the right), the **monitor**, a row with Fit, **Undo** and
**Redo**, a round **Play** button, the **lanes** with a centred playhead (drag the lanes to
scrub), Zoom out and in, and the **bottom strip** of tools.

## The bottom strip

With nothing selected: **Media**, **Text**, **Effects**, **Filters**, **Transitions**,
**Captions**, **Speak**, **Project**. Each opens a sheet that slides up; swipe down to close it.

- **Media**: an **Import media** button and the bin. Tap a file to put it on the timeline at
  the playhead.
- **Text**: **Add a title**, or a title in one of the looks.
- **Effects**, **Filters**, **Transitions**: a search field and the catalogue. Effects and
  transitions go on the selected clip, so select one first.
- **Captions** and **Speak**: the same Captions and Text to speech sheets as the desktop. See
  [auto-captions](/guides/auto-captions) and [text to speech](/guides/text-to-speech).
- **Project**: the project's facts and a **Modify** button for the frame and rate.

With a clip selected the strip changes to **Back**, **Split**, **Delete**, then the tools for
that kind of clip: **Edit** (transform, blend, remove background), **Text**, **Filter**,
**Speed**, **Audio**, **Adjust**, **Effects**, **Transitions**, **Duplicate**, **Freeze**,
**Reverse**, **Mirror**, **Rotate**, **Captions**, **Speak**, and **More** for the rest of the
clip menu.

## What is different

- Split cuts the selected clip at the playhead; there is no razor.
- Undo and Redo are the two buttons beside the monitor.
- Settings has no Remote (Automation) and no Version page, and from 0.2.8 no Shortcuts or Scale.
  Updates come from the APK or IPA on GitHub Releases.
- The start screen has no Open project and no location picker on Android: projects live where
  the app puts them.
- Export has no Browse: on Android the file goes to the phone's Movies folder, where the gallery
  shows it.

## Known problems in 0.2.7

- **iPhone and iPad: Import media does nothing**, so no clip can be brought in. Fixed in 0.2.8.
- **Android phones without Vulkan**: the app closes as it opens. 0.2.8 falls back to OpenGL ES.
- **Android**: switching dark mode, the font size or the language while the app is open closes
  it. Fixed in 0.2.8.
- **Titles in Thai, Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hindi, Bengali or Korean** draw as boxes on phones.
  Fixed in 0.2.8.

## New in 0.2.8

> [!NOTE]
> **New in 0.2.8 (coming in the next release).** Everything in this section is on the app's
> main branch and not in 0.2.7.

- **The lanes glide.** A flick along the lanes carries on after your finger lifts and slows to a
  stop, as other lists on the phone do.
- **Pinch to zoom** the lanes, with the same limits as the zoom buttons. The zoom stays centred
  on the playhead.
- **A tap selects without seeking.** Tapping a clip selects it and leaves the playhead where it
  was, so the clip no longer jumps away from your thumb.
- **Frame step buttons** either side of Play step one frame back or on; hold one to keep
  stepping. The timecode shows the frame you are on.
- **New tools on the strip**: **Voiceover** (the [Voiceover panel](/guides/record-a-voiceover)
  as a sheet) and **Canvas** (the [background and Fit or Fill](/guides/vertical-video)) on the
  main strip; **Canvas**, **Audio effects** and **Enhance** on a clip's strip.
- **Import on iPhone and iPad** asks **Photos** or **Files**. What you pick is copied into the
  app's Documents, which the Files app now shows (projects, Imported and Exports). A finished
  export goes into Photos and stays in Exports.
- **Captions**: the Captions sheet can pick and write `.srt` files on a phone. See
  [auto-captions](/guides/auto-captions#import-and-export-srt).
- **Settings** fills the screen, with its pages as tabs and every model row whole. New project
  asks a phone for no location, and the Export sheet stays inside the system's bars.

## Install
