On a phone, and in any window narrower than about 860 pixels, Concat shows a compact editor
rather than a shrunken desktop. Same engine, same project files, same AI features.

## The layout

Top to bottom: a **top bar** (close project on the left, the project's name in the middle, the
Settings gear and **Export** on the right), the **monitor**, a row with Fit, **Undo** and
**Redo**, a round **Play** button with a frame back and a frame on either side (hold one to keep
stepping; the timecode shows the frame you are on), the **lanes** with a centred playhead, Zoom
out and in, and the **bottom strip** of tools.

The lanes are made for a thumb. Drag them to scrub; a flick carries on after your finger lifts and
slows to a stop. Pinch to zoom, with the same limits as the zoom buttons, centred on the playhead.
A tap on a clip selects it and leaves the playhead where it was.

## The bottom strip

With nothing selected: **Media**, **Audio**, **Text**, **Effects**, **Filters**, **Adjustments**,
**Transitions**, **Captions**, **Speak**, **Voiceover**, **Canvas**, **Project**. Each opens a
sheet that slides up; swipe down to close it.

- **Media**: an **Import media** button and the bin. Tap a file to put it on the timeline at
  the playhead.
- **Audio**: your own sounds and voices; see [your sounds and voices](/guides/sounds-and-voices).
- **Text**: **Add a title**, or a title in one of the looks.
- **Effects**, **Filters**, **Transitions**: a search field and the catalogue. Effects and
  transitions go on the selected clip, so select one first.
- **Adjustments**: **Add adjustment layer**, **Import a LUT** and your LUTs, each laid at the
  playhead. See [Adjustments](/guides/library#adjustments).
- **Captions** and **Speak**: the same Captions and Text to speech panels as the desktop, as
  sheets. See [auto-captions](/guides/auto-captions) and [text to speech](/guides/text-to-speech).
  The Captions sheet can pick and write `.srt` files.
- **Voiceover**: the [Voiceover panel](/guides/record-a-voiceover) as a sheet.
- **Canvas**: the [background and Fit or Fill](/guides/vertical-video).
- **Project**: the project's facts and a **Modify** button for the frame and rate.

With a clip selected the strip changes to **Back**, **Split**, **Delete**, then the tools for
that kind of clip: **Edit** (transform, blend, remove background), **Canvas**, **Text**,
**Filter**, **Adjust**, **Effects**, **Speed**, **Audio**, **Audio effects**, **Mask** (see
[mask a clip](/guides/mask)), **Enhance**, **Transitions**, **Duplicate**, **Freeze**,
**Reverse**, **Mirror**, **Rotate**, **Captions**, **Speak**, and **More** for the rest of the
clip menu. A selected filter or adjustment layer gets the colour and effects sheets a picture clip
has, beside its strength.

## What is different

- Split cuts the selected clip at the playhead; there is no razor.
- Undo and Redo are the two buttons beside the monitor.
- Settings fills the screen, with its pages as tabs and every model row whole. It has no
  Automation, no Version, no Shortcuts and no Scale page or row. Updates come from the APK or IPA
  on GitHub Releases.
- The home page has no Open project and New project asks for no location: projects live where
  the app puts them.
- Export has no Browse. On Android the file goes to the phone's Movies folder, where the gallery
  shows it. On an iPhone or iPad it is added to Photos and kept in the app's **Exports** folder.
- **Import on iPhone and iPad** asks **Photos** or **Files**. What you pick is copied into the
  app's Documents, which the Files app shows (projects, Imported and Exports).
- On Android, video decodes on the phone's hardware decoder (MediaCodec) for 8-bit H.264, HEVC,
  VP9 and AV1; ten-bit HEVC and anything the decoder refuses fall back to the processor.

## Known problems

- **Android**: no automated check has started the arm64 APK, since the release's emulators are
  x86_64. A phone with no Vulkan driver opens the app on OpenGL ES, which has only run on an
  emulator so far. If the app does not open, [say so](https://github.com/jub0t/Concat/issues)
  with your phone's model.
- **iOS and iPadOS** is a beta, installed by sideloading.

## Install

Android: the APK from the [download picker](/#download); allow installs from this source the
first time. iOS and iPadOS: a beta IPA, sideloaded. See [install](/guides/install).
