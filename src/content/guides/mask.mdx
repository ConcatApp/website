A mask keeps one shape of a clip's picture and lets the lanes below show through the rest. It is
the clip's own setting, as in CapCut, not an effect, and it works on video and image clips.

## Add a mask

Select the clip and open the Inspector's **Video** tab, **Basic** page. The **Mask** section has a
row of shapes:

| Shape         | Keeps                                                          |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **None**      | The whole picture. Picking it takes the mask off               |
| **Linear**    | One side of a straight line                                    |
| **Mirror**    | A band between two parallel lines; **Thickness** sets how wide |
| **Circle**    | The inside of a circle or an ellipse                           |
| **Rectangle** | The inside of a rectangle; **Roundness** rounds its corners    |
| **Heart**     | The inside of a heart                                          |
| **Star**      | The inside of a star                                           |

**Invert** keeps the outside of the shape instead. Under the shapes, **Position X** and
**Position Y** place it, **Width** and **Height** size it, **Rotation** turns it and **Feather**
softens its edge.

While the section is open, the monitor outlines the mask on the selected clip, with grips to move
it, size it one way or both, turn it and soften it.

## Animate it

Every number in the section has a diamond, so a mask can move, grow, turn or soften over the clip
like any other knob; see [keyframes](/guides/keyframes). While a clip has a mask, the Keyframes
pane gets a row for each of its numbers.

## What it covers

The mask is cut from the picture as you see it: after the crop and, on a
[stabilized](/guides/stabilize) clip, after the steadying, so it holds still with the steadied
picture. It combines with a [background removal](/guides/remove-a-background), the crop, the
clip's opacity and a wipe. The [canvas](/guides/vertical-video) behind a clip on the main lane
is never masked: it shows through where the mask takes the picture away. The monitor and the
export draw the mask the same way.

A project from 0.2.4 that used the old Mask effects (Circle, Rectangle and Linear) opens with
them turned into the clip's mask, knobs and keys carried over, and a notice says so.

## On a phone

**Mask** is on a selected clip's strip. Its sheet is the shapes as a row of tiles, then Invert,
Feather, Rotation and, for a rectangle, Roundness. Where the mask sits and how big it is are
dragged on the monitor with the same grips.

## From a script

`updateClip` sets or clears a clip's mask, and `setClipKey` keys its numbers (`maskX`, `maskWidth`
and the rest). See [Edit commands](/docs/api/edits#updateclip) and
[Types](/docs/api/types#clipmask).
