The Library's **Audio** page, next to Media, keeps what you want in more than one project. Its
left column has two groups: **Sounds** (My sounds, and Concat sounds) and **Voices** (Voices and
Styles). On a phone, **Audio** on the bottom strip opens the same page as a sheet, with the
shelves as chips.

## My sounds

A sound library of your own. **Import sounds** (or sound files dropped on the window while the
page is up) copies each file into Concat's own data folder, so the shelf survives the original
being moved or deleted, and it is there in every project.

- **Click** a sound to hear it. Its progress runs along the row.
- **The plus** adds it at the playhead, on the first lane with room.
- **Drag** it onto a lane to put it exactly there.

Either way the sound is first copied into the project's `audio` folder and imported into its bin,
once, so the project owns its copy. Removing a sound from the library takes nothing out of a
project that already uses it.

## Concat sounds

A shelf of free sounds and music (CC0) in five categories: Whoosh, Impact, Riser, Ambience and
Music. Nothing is fetched until you press **Load Concat sounds**; each sound would then download
the first time you play or use it, and is checked before it is kept.

> [!NOTE]
> In 0.2.8 the list has not been published yet, so after **Load Concat sounds** the shelf says
> "Sound library coming soon". Your own sounds work without it.

## Voices

**Voices** lists the voices you have saved, then the built-in speakers of the voice models you
have downloaded, each with a round picture or initial. Click one to open
[Text to speech](/guides/text-to-speech) on that voice, with a model that can read it. With no
voice model downloaded, the page says to get one in Settings › Speech.

To save a voice of your own: in Text to speech, turn on **Use sample voice**, pick the recording,
type a name under **Save this voice** (a picture is optional) and press **Save**. Concat keeps the few seconds
the models listen to as a file of its own on this device, so a clone never depends on a long video
in one project. A saved voice is in the voice list of every project, read by Pocket TTS or
Chatterbox Turbo.

## Styles

A style is a voice with Concat's own audio effects on it: **Chipmunk**, **Robot**, **Telephone**,
**Megaphone**, **Radio** and **Giant**. Pick one and Text to speech names it under **Style**; the
read lands with those effects on its clip, where you can change them like any other
[audio effect](/guides/clean-up-a-voice#the-effect-chain).
