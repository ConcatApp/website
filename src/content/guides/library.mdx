Concat ships 170+ effects, filters, transitions and text animations, rendered on the GPU and
shown live in the monitor. They live on three Library pages. What a click does differs by page,
which is the thing that trips people up.

| Page            | Single click                                                  | Double-click or the card's plus               | Needs                                                                     |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Filters**     | Auditions the look over the whole picture, without placing it | Lays the look down as a layer at the playhead | Nothing selected                                                          |
| **Effects**     | Adds the effect to the selected clip                          | Same                                          | One selected video, image or text clip (audio effects: a clip with sound) |
| **Transitions** | Puts the transition on the cut between two selected clips     | Same                                          | Two selected clips that meet on the same track                            |

## Filters

Filters are colour looks: Everyday, Cinematic, Portrait, Food, Scenery, Retro, Film, Black &
White, Vibe, Tone, Clean, Color, and an **Imported** shelf for your own LUTs.

Click a card and the look appears over the picture so you can compare; click it again to take
it off. The first time, a toast explains: "Showing the look over the picture. Double-click the
card, or its plus, to lay it on the timeline."

A laid-down filter is a **layer clip**: it treats everything under it for as long as it runs.
Trim it on the timeline to change what it covers; stack two for a combined grade. Select the
layer and its **Filter** tab has a **Strength** slider and a **Ramp in** and **Ramp out**.

**Import a LUT (.cube)**, on the Filters toolbar, reads a `.cube` table from Resolve or anywhere
else and files it under Imported.

## Effects

The Effects page has two headings in its left column: **Video Effects** (Basic, Blur, Distort,
Glitch, Light, Stylize and more) and **Audio Effects** (Voice, Space, Tone). Select a clip on
the timeline first, then click a card. The Inspector jumps to the clip's **Effects** tab (or
**Audio › Sound** for an audio effect), where the effect sits in the clip's chain with its knobs.

The chain runs top to bottom. Reorder with the chevrons, bypass one with its eye, remove it with
the cross. Any knob with a diamond can be keyframed; see [keyframes](/guides/keyframes). The
Effects tab also has an **Effect library** of its own, with search, a shelf picker and a
**Favourites** star filter: hover a tile in either library and press its star to favourite it.

Chroma key, luma key, colour wheels, curves, contrast, saturation and white balance are effects
too; the first three are also built into every clip's Video tab, under **Remove background**
and **Adjust**.

## Transitions

1. Select the two clips that meet, in either order (<kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd>-click the
   second).
2. Open the Library's **Transitions** page and click a card: Dissolve, Wipe, Slide, Zoom, Spin,
   Glitch, Light, Blur and the rest.

The transition sits on the cut. A small button at the head of the second clip opens it; drag it
to change the duration, or select it and use the **Duration** knob in the Inspector, where
**Remove** also lives. Both clips need at least a frame of handle; the app says so when there is
no room.

## Your own effects

Effects are packages, and a package of your own goes in Concat's effects folder. The Effects
page gets a **Reload custom effects** button once one is there, so a package written or dropped
in while the app runs shows up without a restart, and one that will not load says why. The
community shares packages in the
[ConcatApp/assets](https://github.com/ConcatApp/assets) repository.
