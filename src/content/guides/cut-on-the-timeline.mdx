## Get around

- **Scrub**: drag on the ruler. Click the timecode under the monitor to type a time.
- **Play**: <kbd class="kbd">Space</kbd>. <kbd class="kbd">←</kbd> and
  <kbd class="kbd">→</kbd> step a frame, with <kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd> ten frames.
  <kbd class="kbd">Home</kbd> and <kbd class="kbd">End</kbd> jump to the ends.
- **Zoom**: <kbd class="kbd">⌘</kbd> + wheel zooms about the pointer;
  <kbd class="kbd">⌘=</kbd> and <kbd class="kbd">⌘-</kbd> step; the fit button at the right of the
  tray shows the whole cut.
- **Scroll**: the wheel scrolls the lanes up and down, <kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd> + wheel (or
  a horizontal wheel) scrolls time. <kbd class="kbd">H</kbd> turns on the pan tool, where a drag
  scrolls instead of selecting. The scrollbar under the lanes shows which part is in view.
- **Preview axis** (<kbd class="kbd">S</kbd>): the monitor shows the frame under the pointer
  as it crosses the lanes, so you can find a moment without scrubbing.

## Select

Click a clip. <kbd class="kbd">Shift</kbd>-click or <kbd class="kbd">⌘</kbd>-click adds to the
selection, on the lanes and on the monitor alike. Drag on empty floor to draw a band.

<kbd class="kbd">⌘A</kbd> selects everything.

## Split

- <kbd class="kbd">⌘B</kbd>, or **Split at playhead** on the tray, cuts every clip under the
  playhead.
- <kbd class="kbd">B</kbd> turns on the **razor**: a hairline follows the pointer, and a click on a
  clip cuts it on that frame. Press <kbd class="kbd">B</kbd> again to put it away.
- **Merge selected** joins two pieces of the same file that meet back into one clip.

## Trim and move

Drag a clip's left or right edge to trim it; drag the clip to move it. **Snap to edges**
(<kbd class="kbd">N</kbd>, on by default) makes edges and the playhead stick to each other.
**Trim follow** on the tray makes the playhead ride the edge you trim, so the monitor shows the
new in or out point while you drag.

## Delete and ripple

<kbd class="kbd">Delete</kbd> removes the selection. What happens to the gap is the **Magnetic
timeline** switch on the tray (also in Settings › General):

- **Off** (the default): a delete leaves a gap, a trim moves only the edge.
- **On**: deleting pulls what follows on the same track into the gap, and trimming an edge
  moves the rest of the track with it.

<kbd class="kbd">Shift+Delete</kbd> closes the gap either way, once.

## Freeze, reverse, mirror, rotate

The menu after Delete on the tray acts on the selected clip:

- **Freeze**: park the playhead inside a picture clip and the frame under it becomes a still.
- **Reverse**: the clip is rendered backwards into the project cache; the clip then shows the
  copy. One at a time.
- **Mirror** flips horizontally. **Rotate** turns a quarter clockwise. Both can be keyframed
  from the Inspector.

## Speed

Select the clip and set **Rate** in the Inspector's Speed section. Rate re-times the clip in
place, so its length on the timeline changes; trim afterwards, not before. For sound, **Change
pitch with speed** decides whether a sped-up voice goes up in pitch (off by default).

## Sound on a video clip

- The thin line across a clip with sound is its **volume**; drag it. It snaps at 100 percent.
- Right-click the clip for **Detach audio**: the sound becomes a clip of its own on a lane
  below, so you can offset it or treat it separately. **Reattach audio** puts a detached pair
  back together.
- **Save Audio As…** in the same menu renders the clip's sound, with its treatments, to a WAV
  and files it under Generated › Processed.
- <kbd class="kbd">M</kbd> mutes the selected clip.

## Tracks

<Figure src={header} alt="A track header" width="20rem">
  Row height, lock, hide, mute, More.
</Figure>

The plus at the left of the tray adds a track, to the top or the bottom. Lanes have no names.
Each header has a row-height picker (Auto, Small, Medium, Large), a lock, an eye, a mute, and a
**More** menu that holds **Delete**. Deleting a track asks first and says how many clips go
with it; it can be undone. Drag the edge of the header column to make it wider.

## Several timelines

The tabs above the tray are timelines. The plus adds one; double-click a tab to rename it; drag
to reorder; hover for the close cross. Each timeline has its own frame, rate and colour, so a
project can hold a 16:9 cut and a 9:16 cut of the same footage, and the Export sheet exports
whichever is active.

## Copy, paste, duplicate

<kbd class="kbd">⌘C</kbd>, <kbd class="kbd">⌘V</kbd> and <kbd class="kbd">⌘D</kbd> work on clips,
including across timelines. A paste lands at the playhead.
