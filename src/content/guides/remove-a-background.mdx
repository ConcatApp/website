Select a video or image clip on the timeline and open the Inspector's **Video** tab, **Basic**
section. Scroll to **Remove background**. It has three modes.

## Auto removal

1. Open **Remove background** and pick **Auto removal**.
2. Set **Subject** to **Auto**, **Person** or **Object**. Person uses a model trained on
   people; Object keeps the one thing the picture is of.
3. The row reads "Fetching the model… N%" the first time (15 MB for Person, 179 MB for Object;
   see [the AI models](/guides/offline-models)), then "Finding the subject… N%".
4. Adjust **Feather** to soften the edge.

Auto removal keeps what the model finds and drops the rest. If nothing is found the clip shows
as shot and the panel says so: try another subject, or custom removal.

## Custom removal

Custom removal starts from the same mask and takes corrections you paint on the monitor:

- **Smart brush** adds the thing under your stroke, **Smart eraser** removes it. Both are
  model-aware, so a loose stroke is enough. The first use fetches the brush model (about 40 MB).
- **Brush** and **Eraser** paint the mask directly, pixel by pixel, for the places the smart
  tools miss.
- **Size** and **Feather** set the stroke. The panel counts your strokes.
- Click **Apply** to end painting.

Strokes draw on the stage in blue (keep) and red (erase) while you paint.

## Chroma key

Shot on a real green or blue screen? Pick **Chroma key**, click the **Color** swatch and pick
the screen colour from the picture, then tune **Similarity**, **Softness** and **Spill** (spill
suppression cleans the green fringe off hair and edges). Green Screen and Blue Screen on the
Effects page are the same thing pre-keyed to that colour.

## What goes behind

The cut-out clip has nothing behind it, so put the new background on a lane below it. Any
picture or video works; so does a shape from the Stickers page, or nothing, for a title card.

## Enhance

Next to Basic, the **Enhance** section restores and enlarges a clip's picture with a small
model (5 MB), into a copy the clip then shows. Tick to start; it runs in the background, one clip
at a time, and the row reads "Enhancing… N%". Untick to show the original again.
