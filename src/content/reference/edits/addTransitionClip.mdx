Places a transition as a clip of its own, on a lane above the pictures it
acts on. Over a cut beneath it, it carries the outgoing picture into the
incoming one; over a single shot, it plays over that shot. It starts at
its package's defaults.

<Api of="command:addTransitionClip" />

```json
{
  "op": "addTransitionClip",
  "transitionId": "concat.push",
  "name": "Push",
  "start": 4.5,
  "duration": 0.8
}
```

The packages are listed by `catalogue.list`; a transition's `kind` is
`transition`.
