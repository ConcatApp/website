Gives some of a title's words a look of their own: a word in another
colour, one heavier. Each field of `patch` is laid over the characters
`start` to `end`; anything it leaves out stays as it was. The title's own
style is untouched, so the rest of its words keep following it.

<Api of="command:styleTextRange" />

`start` and `end` count characters (Unicode scalar values), not bytes,
the way most languages count a string; `end` is one past the last. Both
are clamped to the words.

```json
{
  "op": "styleTextRange",
  "clipId": "c7",
  "start": 6,
  "end": 11,
  "patch": { "color": "#ffcc00", "fontWeight": 700 }
}
```

The runs are kept on the title's [TextStyle](/docs/api/types/text-style) as `runs`
([TextRun](/docs/api/types/text-run)).
