Replaces two or more video clips on one track with a file the host has
already rendered from them, as one undo step. The earliest clip keeps its
id, track and span; properties baked into the render are reset. The window's
**Merge selected clips** renders the file and then sends this.

<Api of="command:consolidateClips" />

**Refused** with fewer than two clips, a clip that is not a video, clips on
different tracks, or an unselected clip inside their span.
