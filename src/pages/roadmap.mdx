Concat has no paywall and never will. Sponsorship is what funds the items below; the
[Sponsor section](/#sponsor) has the tiers.

## Next

- Raise capital for Concat to assure continued development and updates.
- Release on the Apple App Store and the Windows Store. Needs capital.
- Create a platform for individual users to share and sell custom effects.

## Costs

What each piece costs to run or to get through the door.

| Item                                                                                                                         | Cost                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Apple Developer Program                                                                                                      | $99 a year          |
| Google Play Store                                                                                                            | $25, one-time fee   |
| The official website: domain, hosting, a database, and a shared community asset store for effects, filters and sound effects | $50 to $100 a month |
| Developer tools, for example Claude                                                                                          | $100 a month        |
