Last updated 3 November 2026.

This policy covers the website at concat.standalones.org. The Concat app is separate: it runs on
your machine and does not upload your footage.

## What this website collects

Nothing of its own. There are no accounts, no forms and no analytics, and the site sets no cookies
of its own.

- **Search.** The search box on the guides and docs runs in your browser. What you type is not sent
  anywhere.
- **The free tools.** Everything under [/tools](/tools) runs in your browser: the video tools
  (compress, convert, trim, extract audio, GIF, inspector) read and write your file on your device
  with the browser's own video encoders, and the calculators and text tools work on what you type.
  No file and nothing you type is uploaded or sent anywhere. The one thing kept is the script length
  counter's draft, which it saves in your browser's local storage so it is still there when you come
  back; it never leaves your device, and emptying the box clears it. The MP3 encoder, when your
  browser has none, is downloaded from this site like any other page file.
- **The download button.** It reads your operating system from your browser to pick the right file.
  That happens on the page and is not sent anywhere.
- **Fonts and images** are served from this site. Nothing is loaded from font services.

## Advertising

This site shows ads through Google AdSense, which loads on every page.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this
  website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your
  visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
  You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at
  [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

Google explains how it uses information from sites that show its ads in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Hosting

Cloudflare serves this website. Like any web host it handles your IP address and browser details to
deliver pages and to protect the site from abuse. See the
[Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Links to other sites

Downloads come from GitHub Releases, so a download is a request to GitHub under the
[GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Links to Discord, X and GitHub take you to those services, under their own policies.
